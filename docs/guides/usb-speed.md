# Your dongle is on a slow USB connection

Your dongle is running at USB 2 speed. USB 2 can carry far less data than the dongle's Wi-Fi link, which can cut your stream quality or cause stutters. You need a USB 3 port, which runs at 5 Gbps or faster.

## How to find a USB 3 port

- **Look at the colour.** USB 3 ports are often blue, teal or red inside. USB 2 ports are usually black or white.
- **Look for a label.** "SS" (SuperSpeed), "SS10", or a "3.0", "3.1" or "3.2" marking means USB 3.
- **Check your PC's manual.** It shows which ports are which.

## What to do

1. Unplug the dongle and plug it into a USB 3 port on your PC.
2. If you use an extension cable or hub, make sure it's also USB 3. A USB 2 cable slows down the whole connection.
3. Run the check again.

If every port shows up as slow, your PC may only have USB 2 ports. A USB 3 expansion card fixes that on a desktop PC.
